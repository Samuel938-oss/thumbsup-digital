Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Who we are

ThumbsUp Digital is a web development and automation business operated by Samuel Pulgar in Cary, North Carolina, USA. In this policy, "we," "us," and "our" mean ThumbsUp Digital.

Contact: samuel@thumbsupdigital.tech · (919) 656-5857

What information we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out one of our forms, including Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads forms and the form on this website, we collect what you submit, such as:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Business name and type of business
  • Your answers to the questions on the form

Technical information. When you visit our website, we collect basic technical information through cookies and analytics tools (such as Google Analytics), like pages visited, device and browser type, and general location. We also use the Meta Pixel and Meta Conversions API to measure how our ads perform.

How we use your information

  • To contact you about your request by phone call, text message, email, or Messenger.
  • To prepare proposals and provide our services to clients.
  • To measure and improve our ads and website.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, and only for the purposes above. These include, for example, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), our website hosting provider, our CRM, our email provider, our payment processor, and our contract-signing tool. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number, we may contact you by phone call or text message about your request. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving text messages. We do not share phone numbers or SMS consent with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Cookies

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser to remember information about your visit. We use them for analytics and ad measurement. You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings (usually under "Privacy" or "Security"). Some parts of the site may work differently if cookies are turned off.

How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as we need it to handle your request or serve you as a client, or as long as the law requires.

Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information we have about you by emailing samuel@thumbsupdigital.tech.
  • You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time, reply STOP to our texts, or unsubscribe from our emails.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no method of sending or storing data online is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our services are for business owners and are not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
